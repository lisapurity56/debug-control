## Exercise 1: Reducing false or invented facts.
1️⃣ Prompt That Produces Errors

Problematic Prompt:



 Result:

Poseidon – Capital of Atlantica

Triton City – Capital of Pacifica

Coral Bay – Capital of Neptunia

Azurepolis – Capital of Marinia

Pearl City – Capital of Oceanis


Improved Prompt:

List five real countries in Africa and their current capitals.

✅ Result:

Nigeria – Abuja

Kenya – Nairobi

Egypt – Cairo

Ghana – Accra

South Africa – Pretoria (administrative capital)

   | Aspect                | Problematic Prompt   | Improved Prompt            |
| --------------------- | -------------------- | -------------------------- |
| Subject               | Fictional place      | Real, verifiable countries |
| Accuracy              | Invented information | Fact-based information     |
| Reliability           | Not trustworthy      | Verifiable and correct     |
